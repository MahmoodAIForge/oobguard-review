With WebGPU (current Chrome or Edge) an hour of video takes a few minutes; on the processor alone it can take over an hour. Browsers decode video slightly differently from the research pipeline, so probabilities can differ a little from the command-line tool; decisions rarely do.

The blackout shown here is a preview drawn over the video. Make copies for sharing with the OOBGuard command-line tool, which checks every frame. OOBGuard is a research prototype, not a medical device, and does not replace human review.
